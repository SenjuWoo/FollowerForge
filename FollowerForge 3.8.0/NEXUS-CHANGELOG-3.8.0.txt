FollowerForge 3.8.0 (beta)

Rebuild your followers with this version. A plugin that is already built keeps its old values.

FIXED IN EVERY FOLLOWER

- Speed multiplier was 0. This is the Creation Kit's "Speed multiplier is zero" message, and in
  game a follower who runs on the spot unless a follower framework resets the value. It is now 100.
- Height was 0. It is now 1.0.
- "Scale with the player" wrote a level multiplier of 16.96 instead of 1.0, so she was about
  seventeen times your level. It is now exactly what you choose.
- A follower placed outdoors overwrote her worldspace (Whiterun, Tamriel, a mod's worldspace) with
  an empty record. Her plugin now carries the real one.
- The build refuses to finish if any of the first three is wrong again.

FIXED IN OPTIONAL FEATURES

- Enemy to Ally: her hostile form never attacked, had a default face instead of hers, and could be
  placed twice. All three fixed.
- Evolution: each phase was added on top of the last, so she gained more than the numbers you set.

FIXED IN THE APP

- "Could not read your setup ... Exception has been thrown by the target of invocation" with every
  list empty. One damaged plugin no longer stops everything: it is skipped, and the status line
  names it.
- Mod Organizer 2: the voice and marriage scan only looked in the game's own Data folder and
  missed every mod. It now reads your mods.
- The app now writes a log: %LOCALAPPDATA%\FollowerForge\logs\ui-*.log
- The scripts shipped inside follower packages no longer carry the name of the PC they were
  compiled on.

NEW

- Open follower... (left sidebar, or Ctrl+K). Choose the rebuild-profile.json inside a follower
  you built before and every page is filled in again, so you can change one thing and rebuild
  instead of starting over. Anything from a mod you no longer have is left out and listed.

NOT CHECKED IN GAME

Every fix above was verified by reading the plugin the app writes, against the base game's own
records. Nobody has recruited a follower built by this version yet, and there is still no exported
RaceMenu head on the build machine, so face conversion, hair placement and tint are unverified as
before. GitHub issue #2 (hair below the head) is still open.
